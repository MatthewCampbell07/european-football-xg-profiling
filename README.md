# ⚽ Patterns Behind the Scoreline: xG-Driven Team Profiling

## Project Overview
Why do some teams dominate but fail to score, while others punch above their weight? This project analyses data from Europe's **Top 5 Leagues** (Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, and Ligue 1) using **Expected Goals (xG)** to move beyond results and uncover the underlying quality of performance.

## Advanced Statistical Approach
Instead of looking at xG in isolation, I applied:
* **Principal Component Analysis (PCA):** To identify the fundamental dimensions of team dominance.
* **K-Means Clustering:** To group teams into distinct performance "profiles."

### Strategic Insights
* **The Dominant Tier:** Bayern Munich and PSG show fundamentally dominant scores in chance creation and opponent suppression.
* **The Inefficiency Trap:** Teams like **Crystal Palace** and **Lens** create high-quality chances but suffer from scoring inefficiency, suggesting a specific need for clinical finishing profiles in recruitment.
* **Efficiency Outliers:** **Holstein Kiel** stands out for remarkable efficiency relative to their xG.

## Tactical & Business Application
This framework provides actionable intelligence for:
1. **Targeted Recruitment:** Identifying clubs that create chances but lack finishers (perfect for loaning out young strikers).
2. **Strategic Planning:** Assessing whether a team's league position is sustainable based on underlying metrics.
